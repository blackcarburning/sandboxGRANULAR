Cello, viola and violin section sustain-vibrato recordings mapped across 17 roots (C2–D6), two dynamic layers (32/96). Numeric pitch centres come from the original SFZ mappings (their filenames use C3 = MIDI 60). Original SFZ layer gains with a common headroom attenuation; 4.5-second stereo PCM16/44.1 kHz samples, 1.5–4.5-second sustain loops with a 200 ms linear seam crossfade. Notes outside the recorded range are pitch-shifted. Originals retained; no endorsement implied.
